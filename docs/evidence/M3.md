## Unverified assumptions
- Truecolour appearance and whether the plasma visibly shimmers behind the board cannot be seen from this headless shell; TestBackend proves per-cell Rgb values, distinct backgrounds and dt-driven change only. The live visual check stays with the human/M4 (N5).
- The live measured-and-clamped `dt` loop compiles and is wired, but no real tty was available to exercise it; the deterministic 16 ms frame contract carries the animation proof.
- Ring and spark beauty/geometry are the coder's choice and are reviewed by eye from the plain-text captures; the structural tests only stop empty or static effects.

## Limitations and known issues
- The shockwave and sparks are clipped to the 35x19 board block, so a ring approaching an edge is cut off rather than drawn over the header/footer.
- The effect RNG has one fixed seed per `App`, so the first merge of every fresh session produces the same spark pattern (required for deterministic captures).
- Effect fixtures and `--render-once` are plain-text symbol captures; 24-bit colours and animation smoothness are not captured by them.
- `make e2e` rewrites `docs/evidence/M3/frame_*.txt`; the committed copies are the gate output.

## Deviations and near-misses
- Round 1 rendered the plasma and then let the outer `Block` paint `BACKGROUND` over the whole frame, erasing the backgrounds. F1 removes that opaque style; the new `plasma_background_reaches_the_frame_buffer` test failed before the fix and passes after it.
- The round-1 evidence log was corrupted by two `ev.sh` calls running concurrently. This file was rebuilt sequentially in round 2; malformed blocks and the misleading "M2 review diff is empty" block were removed (F3).
- `docs/reviews/M2.md` was untracked at the base commit, so `git diff 0a00859..HEAD` shows it as an addition. Commit 53da044 contains the reviewer's file verbatim; the working-tree and committed hashes match.
- No game-rule, RNG, dependency or protected-file changes were made; the M2 snapshot fixtures are byte-identical.
