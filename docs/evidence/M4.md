## Unverified assumptions
- Live 24-bit truecolour and whether the plasma visibly shimmers behind the board cannot be seen from this headless shell. The snapshot and effect tests prove per-cell `Color::Rgb` values, distinct backgrounds and `dt`-driven change only; the live visual check stays with the human (Q1/N5).
- The live measured-and-clamped `dt` loop compiles and is wired but was never exercised under a real tty here; the deterministic 16 ms frame contract carries the animation proof.
- The `script` pty in this environment does not paint the target 100x36 layout; the `TestBackend` snapshots carry the visible-layout proof. The pty run proves only setup/restore and exit 0.
- The committed captures are plain-text symbol captures: they prove which characters and styles reach the buffer, not terminal colour or animation smoothness.
- Whether `ratatui::style::Color::Rgb` is emitted as truecolour depends on the terminal emulator; the crate only writes SGR sequences.

## Limitations and known issues
- The shockwave and sparks are clipped to the 35x19 board block, so a ring approaching an edge is cut off rather than drawn over the header/footer.
- The effect RNG has one fixed seed per `App`, so the first merge of every fresh session produces the same spark pattern (required for deterministic captures).
- `make e2e` rewrites the three captures; they are committed and `diff -u`'d against the fixtures, so drift fails the target.
- The agent shell is headless (no tty), so the human has to confirm truecolour, live restore and a real BEST-across-restart run.
