## Unverified assumptions
- The G14/G15 values come from `f64` arithmetic in `ScoreCount::shown`; the literals match at every tested step in this toolchain (rustc 1.99.1). A different `f64` implementation could in principle shift a floor at an untested `t`, but the clamped 450 ms branch and the ten tested steps are exact here.
